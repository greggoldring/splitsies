Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Splitsies: Track Cycling Splits (“the App”) is published by Gregg Goldring. This policy describes how the App handles your information.

Data collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data about you. The App has no accounts and no servers, and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind. We never see your race times, and nothing in the App identifies you or your device.

Data stored on your device

Stopwatch times, race history, air pressure readings, and any custom venues you create are stored only on your device, using the App’s local storage. This data is never uploaded anywhere.

Weather lookups

The App records the air pressure at each lap. On devices with a barometer, this is read from the device’s own sensor and needs no internet connection. Devices without a barometer — including all iPads — cannot do this, so for those the App can instead look up the air pressure recorded for the venue you selected.

These lookups are requested from Open-Meteo, a third-party weather service. Each request contains only two things: the approximate location of the venue — rounded to one decimal place of latitude and longitude, an area on the order of 10 kilometres across — and the date of your session. It does not contain your name, your race times, your device’s location, or any identifier for you or your device.

The App does not use GPS or location services at all, and never asks for location permission. The coordinates sent are those of the velodrome you picked from the venue list, coarsened before they leave your device, and are not a location reading taken from your device.

As with any internet request, Open-Meteo can see the IP address the request came from. Their terms are at open-meteo.com/en/terms.

You can turn these lookups off completely in Settings → Privacy & Offline → Weather API lookups. With them off, the App makes no internet requests of any kind. On devices with a barometer, pressure recording continues to work offline.

Children’s privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. No personal information is requested, stored, or transmitted. The weather lookups described above carry no information about the person using the App.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or the App, you may contact the developer at the support contact provided in the App Store listing for Splitsies: Track Cycling Splits.
